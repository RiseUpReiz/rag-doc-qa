# Halden Marine Systems — Vendor FAQ

## How long does vendor onboarding take?

Vendor onboarding takes 10 business days from receipt of a complete
application, including the security review.

## What certification do vendors need?

All vendors that handle company data must hold a current ISO 27001
certification.
