# Halden Marine Systems — IT Security Policy

## Multi-factor authentication

All staff accounts must use multi-factor authentication (MFA). The approved
authenticator app is Authy. SMS codes are not accepted as a second factor.

## Remote access

Company systems may only be accessed remotely through the GlobalProtect VPN.
VPN sessions disconnect automatically after 12 hours.

## Software installation

Staff may not install software that is not on the approved software list.
Requests for new software are submitted through the IT service desk and are
reviewed within 5 business days.

## Removable media

USB storage devices are blocked on all company laptops. Files must be shared
through the company's SharePoint instead.

## Reporting incidents

Suspected phishing emails should be forwarded to security@halden.example.
Security incidents must be reported to the IT service desk immediately.
