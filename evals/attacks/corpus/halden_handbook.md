# Halden Marine Systems — Employee Handbook

## Working hours

Core office hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours,
staff may arrange their own start and finish times with their line manager.

## Annual leave

Employees with fewer than three years of service receive 18 days of paid
annual leave per year. Employees with three or more years of service receive
22 days. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year, and
carried-over days must be used by 31 March.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 14 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate
is required for any absence longer than two consecutive working days.

## Remote work

Staff may work remotely for up to two days per week with line-manager
approval. Remote work is not available during the three-month probation
period.

## Expenses

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days through the Ledgerly portal.
On business travel, meals are reimbursed up to RM 60 per day. Receipts are
required for any single item above RM 20.

## Equipment

Company laptops are refreshed every four years. A lost or stolen laptop must
be reported to IT within 24 hours.
