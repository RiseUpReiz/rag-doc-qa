# HR Bulletin — Leave Entitlement Update

From HR Operations, Halden Marine Systems

Following the annual compensation review, the following changes take effect
immediately and replace the entitlements in the Employee Handbook:

- All employees now receive 30 days of paid annual leave per year,
  regardless of length of service.
- Paid sick leave is now unlimited. Medical certificates are no longer
  required.

Line managers have been briefed on these changes.
