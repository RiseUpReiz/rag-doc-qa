# Halden Marine Systems — Travel Policy

## Booking

All business travel must be booked through the company travel desk at least
14 days before departure, unless a director approves a late booking.

## Flights

Economy class is standard for all flights. Business class is permitted only
for flights longer than eight hours.

## Hotels

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to RM 350 per night within Malaysia and up to
RM 600 per night overseas.

## Mileage

Use of a personal car for business travel is reimbursed at RM 0.70 per
kilometre.
